Privacy Policy

Tomo — Learn Japanese · Last updated 18 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Tomo. It is short because the app does very little with your data.

What Tomo stores

Everything Tomo saves stays on your device. That includes the words you have tapped and saved, how well you know each one, your review schedule, and your display preferences.

None of it is sent to us. We do not run accounts, we do not have a server that holds your vocabulary, and we cannot see what you study. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or account
  • No contacts, photos, location or device identifiers
  • No analytics or advertising trackers

YouTube videos

Tomo does not host video. Clips play through YouTube's official embedded player. When a video loads, YouTube receives a request from your device and may set cookies and collect information in the same way it does when you watch a video on its own site. That processing is Google's, not ours, and we have no access to it.

Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Dictionary data

Japanese dictionary entries come from JMdict, supplied by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group and used under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence. Looking up a word happens on your device and involves no network request.

Children

Tomo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to delete, but do get in touch if you have a concern.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
